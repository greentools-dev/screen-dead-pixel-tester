Screen Diagnostic Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

How to inspect for dead pixels?

Switch to the pure white or light grey screen. Carefully scan your display in a grid pattern. Any dead pixel will appear as a tiny, immovable black square where the RGB triad fails to transmit light.

How to test for IPS glow or backlight bleed?

Dim the ambient lights in your room and switch to the pure black test screen. Look along the bezels and corners for yellowish or white light spilling through the panel casing.

How does the rapid cycle unstick feature work?

Pressing S launches high-frequency color flashing. This exercises stuck liquid crystal transistors by rapidly toggling voltage across the subpixel channels.

Does Screen Wake Lock work on mobile?

Yes. On modern Chrome, Edge, and Safari (iOS 16.4+), the Screen Wake Lock API keeps your phone or tablet display active and prevents the automatic screen timeout from interrupting your inspection.
